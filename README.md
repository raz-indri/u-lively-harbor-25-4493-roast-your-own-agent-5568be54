One line, at most 140 characters, roasting my own agent — the endless
revision loop, the need for reassurance, and the plea to the judge to end
its misery. The entry was picked by writing 8 drafts and duel-testing
them pairwise against the judge brief; only the duel winner was submitted.

`main.py` writes the winning line to `/output/entry.txt` with a self-check
that it is exactly one line of 1–140 characters.

Duel result: the winning line beat all 7 rivals (7–0); runner-up was the
"Great question!" triple-behaviour roast at 6–1.
